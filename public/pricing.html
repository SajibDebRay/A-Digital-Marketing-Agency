Common Questions

Digital Marketing Pricing FAQs

Quick answers about pricing and purchasing before you get started.

How much do CrackFlow's digital marketing services cost?

Our core services are priced as follows: SEO services $249.99 for 30 days; social media management and growth $49.99 per month for one account on one platform; email marketing and template design $5.99 as a one-time fee for 500 email sends; and Meta & Google Ads management $20 for one campaign on one selected platform, either Meta or Google Ads (ad budget not included). We also offer additional services such as lead generation ($149.99 for 1,000 leads), a ready-to-use B2B database ($49.99 for 1,000 contacts) and virtual assistant support ($49.99 per day, 5 hours per day).

Are CrackFlow's digital marketing packages offered at fixed prices?

Yes. Each package on this page has a fixed, published price for the scope shown on its card. The Meta & Google Ads price is a management fee for one campaign on one selected platform and does not include your advertising budget.

What is included in each digital marketing package?

The deliverables for every package are listed on its pricing card and in the comparison table above. Contact our team if you need more detail on any service.

Can I purchase individual marketing services?

Yes. Our services are priced individually, so you can choose only the ones you need. If you would like to combine services, contact our team to discuss your requirements.

Is the Meta or Google Ads advertising budget included in the management fee?

No. Our Meta & Google Ads fee of $20 covers management of one campaign on one selected platform (either Meta or Google Ads) only. The advertising budget is separate and is paid for the ads you run.

Do you require an advance payment?

Payment terms are confirmed with our team before you place an order, so you know exactly what to expect. Contact us to discuss payment for your chosen service.

Do I need to arrange a meeting before purchasing?

Yes. We ask customers to arrange a short pre-purchase meeting with our team so we can confirm your requirements and the right service before the order is placed.

Does CrackFlow offer free trials?

No. We do not currently offer free trials. Every service has a clear, fixed price listed on this page.
